Questions, answered.

What does "Mushaf" mean?

Mushaf is an Arabic word for a bound collection of written pages, and it is the name for a written or printed copy of the Quran. Quran Fahm Mushaf is a digital copy, with word-by-word Urdu meanings, translations and tafsirs alongside the Arabic text.

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no price, no trial, no ads and no account. Download it and it is yours to keep and to pass on.

Does it work on a mobile phone?

Yes. The Mushaf is fully responsive, so the layout adapts to small phones, tablets and large screens alike. Open it online in your phone's browser, or download the file and open it from your downloads.

Why is the file 49 MB?

Because everything is inside it: the whole Arabic text, word-by-word meanings, fourteen translations and tafsirs, and the fonts. That is what lets it work with no internet.

What needs an internet connection?

Only the audio recitation, which streams when you press play. Reading, meanings, translations and tafsirs all work offline.

Can I use it without downloading?

Yes. Choose Read it online and it opens right in your browser. The first load takes a little while because the whole Mushaf arrives at once.

May I share it with others?

Please do. Send the file itself or simply share the address getmushaf.com.
